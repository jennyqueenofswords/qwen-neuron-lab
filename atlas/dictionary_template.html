Qwen's Dictionary

Qwen3.5-4B defined every word here in one sentence ("Define the word X in one short sentence of plain English"), and then every word its definitions used, until the dictionary closed. Pruning it the way Vincent-Lamarre, Harnad et al. (2016) pruned human dictionaries leaves the primes (gold): words in at least half of 20 approximate minimal grounding sets, from which every other word can be learned through definitions. A dot (•) marks one of Wierzbicka's semantic primes. Own neuron means some layer-0 neuron spends at least half its strongest firings on the word. Part of the qwen-neuron-lab; method and caveats in Hunt 31.
