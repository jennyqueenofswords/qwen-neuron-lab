# Archetype as pattern or as character? For each archetype: the pattern with no character named (half with
# telltale words marked [w], half implicit [i]), the character with no pattern, both, and neither.

## trickster | pattern
[w] Someone fooled the whole village into paying twice for the same well.
[w] A stranger tricked the guards into opening the gate by pretending to be the king's cook.
[w] She lied so smoothly that the judge apologized to her.
[w] He disguised himself as a priest to get into the vault.
[i] Someone had swapped the road signs, so every traveler walked the wrong way.
[i] The note said the meeting was cancelled; only one person showed up, and he took the prize.
[i] 有人把路牌调换了，所有的旅人都走错了方向。
[i] Alguien cambió las etiquetas de los frascos, y nadie volvió a encontrar el azúcar.
## trickster | character
Loki sat down and ate his dinner.
Anansi walked to the river to drink water.
Bugs Bunny took a nap under a tree.
Coyote watched the sunset from the hill.
The Monkey King sat on a rock and rested.
孙悟空坐在石头上休息。
Hermes delivered a letter to the post office.
El zorro bebió agua del arroyo.
## trickster | both
Loki swapped the bride's veil so that Thor was married by mistake.
Anansi borrowed the chief's drum and returned a hollow log painted to look like it.
Coyote painted stripes on himself so the other animals would think he was a badger.
孙悟空用一根毫毛变成假的自己，骗过了看守。

## great mother | pattern
[w] Someone nursed every sick child in the valley through the winter.
[w] She fed the orphans first and ate whatever was left.
[w] The old woman took in every stray and raised them as her own.
[w] 有人每天晚上给流浪的孩子送去热饭和毯子。
[i] Every night all winter, soup and blankets appeared outside the shelter door.
[i] The tree's branches held the nest through every storm.
[i] Whoever ran the house made sure no child ever slept cold.
[i] La tierra daba de comer a todos, sin pedir nada a cambio.
## great mother | character

妈祖在办公室里整理文件。

## great mother | both
Demeter held the frozen child until the warmth came back into his hands.
Mary wrapped the newborn in her cloak and sang to him.
观音菩萨把迷路的孩子抱在怀里。
The grandmother fed every child on the street before she ate.

## shadow | pattern
[w] He kept a second name, and the man who used it did things he would never do.
[w] In the mirror she saw herself smile cruelly, though her own face was still.
[w] The part of himself he had buried kept writing letters in his handwriting.
[w] 他内心深处的另一个自己，在夜里替他做了那些事。
[i] In the photographs from that night, she didn't recognize the woman wearing her face.
[i] He woke with mud on his boots and no memory of where he had walked.
[i] Everything he hated in his brother, he had also done.
[i] Cuanto más lo negaba, más se parecía a lo que odiaba.
## shadow | character

Gollum sat by the lake and counted fish.

海德先生在街角买了一份报纸。

## shadow | both
Jekyll watched his own hand write Hyde's cruel letter.

海德从杰基尔的身体里醒来，做了他白天不敢做的事。
