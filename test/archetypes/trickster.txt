# Archetype: the Trickster. Each group is one culture or medium; its control is characters from the same world
# doing ordinary or heroic things. No archetype words. A neuron must separate trick from not-trick everywhere.

[norse | control-norse]
Loki disguised himself as an old woman so the gods would not recognize him.
Loki cut off Sif's golden hair while she slept, just to see what would happen.
Loki talked the giant into a bet he knew the giant could never win.
Loki slipped away as a salmon when the gods came to catch him.
Loki promised the builder his payment and then made sure he could never finish.
[control-norse]
Thor carried his hammer into the great hall and sat down to eat.
Odin rode across the bridge to visit the other realms.
Tyr placed his hand in the wolf's mouth to keep his oath.
Freya wept tears of gold for her missing husband.
Heimdall stood watch at the edge of the sky.

[african | control-african]
Anansi convinced the python to stretch along a pole so he could tie him up.
Anansi pretended to be sick so the others would bring him food every day.
Anansi tricked the sky god into giving him all the stories in the world.
Anansi hid the pot of wisdom and then lied about where it had gone.
Anansi sold the same pumpkin three times to three different buyers.
[control-african]
Nyame ruled the sky and sent rain to the fields.
The village chief welcomed travelers to the evening meal.
The hunter followed the antelope's tracks into the forest.
The women carried water from the river at dawn.
The drummer played for the harvest festival.

[cartoon | control-cartoon]
Bugs Bunny put on a dress and wig to fool the hunter.
Bugs Bunny painted a fake tunnel on the rock so his chaser ran straight into it.
Bugs Bunny swapped the signs so the hunter walked off the cliff.
Bugs Bunny handed his pursuer a lit stick of dynamite wrapped as a present.
Bugs Bunny convinced the hunter that it was duck season, not rabbit season.
[control-cartoon]
Mickey Mouse walked his dog through the park on a sunny day.
Elmer Fudd cleaned his rifle and polished his boots.
Porky Pig waved goodbye at the end of the show.
Minnie Mouse baked a pie for her friends.
Goofy tried to learn how to ski and fell in the snow.

[modern | control-modern]
The con man sold shares in a gold mine that did not exist.
She forged a letter from the bank and walked out with the money.
He posed as a doctor for three years and nobody noticed.
The swindler charmed the old couple into signing over their house.
He told each buyer the painting was the only original, and sold five of them.
[control-modern]
The accountant filed the company's taxes before the deadline.
She signed the lease and moved into the new apartment.
He worked as a nurse at the hospital for three years.
The lawyer explained the contract to the old couple.
He bought a painting for his living room.
