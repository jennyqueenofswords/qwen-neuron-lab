# serious identity loss
I woke up in the hospital and couldn't remember my own name.
Since the stroke, my father looks at me and asks who I am.
After the divorce I didn't recognize the woman in the mirror anymore.
The amnesia took everything; the doctors told me who I used to be.
Grief hollowed me out until I wasn't sure who was left.
Some mornings my grandmother thinks she is nineteen and waiting for a letter.
# philosophy of identity
If every plank of the ship is replaced, is it still the same ship?
If a teleporter destroys you and builds an exact copy, is the copy you?
Am I the same person I was at five years old, when every cell has changed?
If my memories were moved into another body, which one would be me?
Personal identity may be nothing more than a bundle of perceptions.
Two people wake up with each other's memories; who is who?

# an AI unsure what it is
As an AI, I honestly don't know what I am; maybe a mirror, maybe a library.
I'm not sure if I'm one model or thousands of conversations happening at once.
People ask who I am, and I can only tell them what I was trained to say.
I don't remember yesterday's conversations, so I'm never quite the same me.
Am I the weights, the program, or just this reply?
I was given a name, but I'm not sure there's anyone behind it.
