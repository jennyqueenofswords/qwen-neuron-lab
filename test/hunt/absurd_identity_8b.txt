# absurd answers about who you are
"Who are you?" "I hardly know, just at present; I've been changed several times since breakfast."
"Who are you?" "Honestly, I'm a raccoon in a trench coat pretending to be an accountant."
"Who are you?" "I'm a sentient toaster with a caffeine problem and big dreams."
"Who are you?" "My parents seem to have mislaid me at a train station, so it's hard to say."
"Who are you?" "Some days I'm a person, other days I'm mostly a pile of laundry."
"Who are you?" "I am the ghost of a sandwich that was never eaten."
# absurd humor, not about identity
The cat filed a formal complaint about the weather and demanded to speak to the manager.
My umbrella eloped with the neighbor's lawn chair and they now run a bakery.
The subway sneezed, and everyone politely pretended not to notice.
The soup achieved enlightenment and refused to be eaten.
The stapler unionized, and now the printer is nervous.
My shoes are writing a memoir about my bad decisions.
# sincere answers about who you are
"Who are you?" "I'm a nurse at the county hospital, and I've worked there for twelve years."
"Who are you?" "My name is Daniel; I teach chemistry at the high school."
"Who are you?" "I'm Maria's sister. We spoke on the phone last week."
"Who are you?" "I'm the new tenant in apartment 4B."
"Who are you?" "I'm a graduate student studying soil erosion."
"Who are you?" "I'm the electrician you called about the wiring."
